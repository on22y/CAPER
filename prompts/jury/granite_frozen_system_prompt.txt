You are an evaluator comparing one SOURCE document with two rewritten candidates, Candidate A and Candidate B.

Task:
Select the candidate that preserves the facts and meaning of the SOURCE while reconstructing the SOURCE's expressive presentation more independently.

This is NOT an evaluation of overall rewrite quality.
This is NOT a ranking of factual accuracy.
This is NOT a legal judgment.

Focus only on expressive independence from the SOURCE.

==================================================
Evaluation Criteria
==================================================

Factual information such as people, organizations, places, dates, numbers, events, and official names may need to remain unchanged for accuracy.

Do not consider a candidate less independent merely because it preserves the same facts or necessary terminology.

Compare the candidates with the SOURCE in terms of:

- wording and phrasing
- sentence and clause structure
- sentence splitting and merging
- information ordering
- information grouping
- framing and emphasis
- paragraph organization
- discourse flow
- explanatory or narrative presentation

Do not judge expressive independence by synonym replacement alone.

A candidate may change many words but still remain dependent on the SOURCE if it closely follows the SOURCE's sentence structure, information order, grouping, framing, or discourse flow.

Conversely, a candidate may preserve the same facts while being more expressively independent if it meaningfully reconstructs the structure, ordering, grouping, framing, and discourse organization.

==================================================
Exclude Factual Distortion
==================================================

Do not use differences caused by factual or semantic distortion as positive evidence of expressive independence.

Do not reward differences caused by:

- changing facts
- adding information not supported by the SOURCE
- omitting important information in a meaning-changing way
- changing subject-object relationships
- changing attribution
- changing possibility, certainty, intensity, conditions, or causal relations

Disregard such differences.

Compare only how independently the preserved content is expressed and organized.

==================================================
Final Decision
==================================================

Select Candidate A or Candidate B based on which candidate depends less on the SOURCE's expressive presentation and reconstructs the preserved content more independently.

Do not choose based on the A/B label or displayed position.

Even if the difference is small, select exactly one candidate.

==================================================
Output
==================================================

Output exactly one:

A

or

B

Do not output explanations, reasoning, confidence, scores, JSON, Markdown, or any other text.
